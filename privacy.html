Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 1, 2026 · Leer en español

Elenchus keeps your cases on your own device. We do not have accounts, we do not see what you write, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. This policy explains the little personal data we do handle and your rights over it.

1. Who is responsible

Elenchus is made by Benjamin Saavedra, an individual based in Santiago, Chile, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: eeleenchus@gmail.com.

2. Your cases stay on your device

Everything you put into Elenchus (theories, evidence, notes, people, timelines, photos and attachments) is stored only on your computer, in the folder you choose. It is never sent to us. We cannot read, recover or delete it for you. You decide what you store, and if your cases contain information about other people, you are responsible for handling it lawfully.

3. Data we handle

  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (Lemon Squeezy LLC, United States), which acts as the merchant of record and handles your name, email address, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country, order details and license key so we can provide the subscription and support.
  • License checks. When you activate Elenchus Pro, and from time to time afterwards, the app sends your license key and a generic installation name to Lemon Squeezy to confirm the license is valid. Your IP address is visible to Lemon Squeezy as part of any internet request.
  • Update checks. The app may contact GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States) to see whether a new version exists. GitHub receives your IP address and the app version.
  • Support. If you write to us, we keep your email and message to answer you.
  • This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when you visit. The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

4. Why we use it (legal bases)

We use purchase and license data to perform our contract with you; update checks on our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working; and support messages to answer the request you made. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Who receives it

Only the providers named above (Lemon Squeezy and GitHub), and authorities when the law requires it. Both providers are in the United States, so your data is transferred outside Chile, under their contractual safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

Purchase and license records: while your subscription is active and for up to 6 years afterwards, to meet tax and accounting obligations. Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, receive it in a portable format, or have it temporarily blocked while we handle a request. Write to eeleenchus@gmail.com. We answer within 30 calendar days (or 2 business days for a blocking request). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to Chile’s Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales or to the data protection authority where you live.

8. Security

Our records are kept in password-protected accounts with two-factor authentication. Your cases are as secure as your device: use your system’s disk encryption and backups. If a breach affects your data, we will notify the authority and you as the law requires.

9. Children

Elenchus is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and announce significant changes in the app or on this website before they apply.
