Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data is processed when you use Elenchus: the desktop application (the "Software"), the website at eeleenchus.github.io (the "Website") and our support. The person responsible for your data (the data controller) is Benjamín Saavedra Álvarez, Santiago, Chile, eeleenchus@gmail.com ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • Your case files never leave your computer. We do not receive, store, read or back up your cases, evidence, notes, attachments or client information.
  • The Software has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • When you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your purchase as Merchant of Record. We receive what it needs to give us to manage your subscription.
  • We never sell your personal data.

2. What we process, and why

We do not process special categories of data about you. Any sensitive information about third parties that you put in your case files stays on your device and is never processed by us.

3. Who else processes data

Lemon Squeezy, LLC (United States) is our reseller and Merchant of Record. It processes payment and billing data under its own privacy policy and acts as an independent controller for payments. Card details go directly to Lemon Squeezy and its payment processors; we never see them.

GitHub, Inc. (United States) hosts the Website, the configuration file and the installers. It may log technical request data under its own privacy policy.

Our email provider (Google) processes the messages you send us.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep data

  • Subscription and billing records: for as long as your subscription is active and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 6 years).
  • Support emails: up to 3 years after the last message.
  • License data held in the Software: on your computer, until you deactivate or uninstall it.

5. Security

Your case files are protected by the security of your own computer; we recommend full-disk encryption (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows).

The Software: - runs its interface in a sandbox without access to your system; - blocks scripts embedded in case files; - encrypts the stored license with your operating system's keychain; - talks to our services only over HTTPS.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data. These include the rights of access, rectification, deletion, objection and portability (for example under Chilean Law No. 19,628, the EU and UK GDPR, or Californian law). You can also withdraw consent where we rely on it, and lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

To exercise them, write to eeleenchus@gmail.com. We will answer within the time the law requires, and in any case within 30 days. Because we do not hold your case files, a request about them cannot be fulfilled by us: you control them directly on your computer.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will publish the new version on the Website with a new effective date. Where the law requires it, the Software will ask you to accept it.
