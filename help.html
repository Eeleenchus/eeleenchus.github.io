Getting started

  1. Install Elenchus from the download page and accept the terms on first launch.
  2. Explore the sample case, State v. Mercer. It doesn’t count toward the free plan, and the short tour walks you through it.
  3. Create your case. Choose New case and pick a template (criminal defense, prosecution, civil or contract) or start blank.
  4. Write the case theories: yours, the opposing one and any alternative a factfinder could accept.
  5. Log the evidence and grade each item. Type a title and press Enter in the quick-add bar, paste a link or image, or drop files onto the window.
  6. Run the theory test. Mark how each item bears on each theory. The Overview then shows the strongest theory, why, and the evidence it depends on.
  7. Export the case memo to Word or PDF to share with your team or prepare for the hearing.

Key ideas

TermWhat it means in Elenchus
Case theoryOne complete account of what happened that, if the factfinder believes it, decides the case. Mark each as ours, opposing or alternative.
Evidence gradeTwo parts: how reliable the source is (A, reliable, to D, doubtful) and how well the information is corroborated (1, confirmed, to 4, unverified). A2 is a reliable source with probably true information.
WeightCalculated from the grade, from ×0 to ×3. It decides how much an item counts in the theory test. You can set an item aside (×0) without deleting it.
ArgumentA conclusion you draw from evidence, such as “the fire was set deliberately”. It only adds what its own evidence doesn’t already account for.
Theory testThe table of every item against every theory. Undermines adds the item’s weight to a theory’s points against; contradicts adds twice its weight. Support never lowers the count.
Points againstThe total for each theory. Fewer is stronger: the strongest theory is the one the record weighs least against, not the one with the most support.
Pivotal evidenceThe item that, if it fell, would change which theory is strongest. Expect the other side to attack it, and protect it.
Probative valueHow much an item tells the theories apart. An item that fits every theory equally proves little, however reliable it is.
Elenchus doesn’t give legal advice. Its results are the arithmetic of the ratings you enter; your judgment decides.

License and billing

Free plan

One active case with every feature, for as long as you like. The sample case doesn’t count. Cases you move to the Trash don’t count.

Buying and activating Pro

  1. Choose a plan on the pricing page, or select Upgrade inside Elenchus.
  2. After checkout you’ll receive an email with your license key.
  3. In Elenchus, open Settings, paste the key under Elenchus Pro and choose Activate.

Moving to another computer

A license works on two computers at a time. To move it, open Settings on the old computer and choose Deactivate, then activate it on the new one.

Working offline

Elenchus re-checks the license every few days when it’s online, and keeps working for up to 30 days without a connection.

Cancelling

Cancel anytime from the link in your receipt email or in the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Payments are non-refundable; see the Refund and Cancellation Policy.

If Pro ends

Your cases are never deleted. You can open, read, print and export all of them. If you have more than one active case, editing pauses until you renew or move cases to the Trash so only one remains.

Your files and backups

  • Cases are saved in Documents › Elenchus. Each case is a folder with its data and attachments. Choose Show in Finder or Show in Explorer in the library to open it.
  • We never receive your cases, so we can’t recover them. Back them up with Time Machine, File History or your firm’s backup.
  • Turn on full-disk encryption: FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows.
  • To share a case, use Export case. The other person opens it with Import.
  • If you keep your library in a synced folder, make sure that service meets your confidentiality duties.

Troubleshooting

“Elenchus is damaged and can’t be opened” on Mac

This is macOS reacting to an app without a paid Apple certificate. Follow the install steps; the Terminal command there clears the warning.

“Windows protected your PC”

Choose More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the installer isn’t signed with a paid Microsoft certificate.

“This license key is not for Elenchus” or “not found”

Copy the key again from your receipt email, without spaces. If it still fails, write to us with your order number.

“The activation limit was reached”

Deactivate the license on a computer you no longer use. If you can’t reach it, write to us and we’ll reset it.

I can’t edit my cases

Your Pro subscription has ended and you have more than one active case. Renew, or move cases to the Trash until only one remains. Nothing is lost either way.
