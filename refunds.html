Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy forms part of the Terms of Service.

Try first, for free

Elenchus has a free plan with every feature included, limited to one active case, for as long as you like. Please use it to decide whether Elenchus is right for you before subscribing to Pro.

No refunds

All payments for Elenchus Pro, monthly or yearly, are final and non-refundable. This includes: - partial billing periods; - time left after you cancel; - periods in which you did not use the Software; - renewals you forgot to cancel.

To the extent permitted by law, including article 3 bis (b) of Chilean Law No. 19,496, the right of withdrawal (derecho de retracto) is expressly excluded.

This does not affect any right to a refund that the mandatory consumer law of your country of residence gives you and that cannot be waived by contract. If such a right applies to you, write to eeleenchus@gmail.com and we will honor it.

Cancel anytime

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time from the link in your receipt email, or from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal.
  • Cancelling stops future charges. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. You will not be charged again.
  • After that, Elenchus returns to the free plan. You never lose your cases: you can open, read, print and export all of them. If you have more than one active case, editing is paused until you renew or keep only one.

Billing errors

If you were charged twice, or charged after a cancellation that had taken effect, write to us within 60 days and we will refund the incorrect charge.

Payments

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and Merchant of Record. If you dispute a valid charge with your bank instead of cancelling, we may deactivate your license key.
