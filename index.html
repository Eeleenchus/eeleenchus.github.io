I.

Every lawyer believes their own theory. The record doesn’t.

Confirmation bias doesn’t stop at the courtroom door. Elenchus gives you a method against it: the analysis of competing hypotheses, used by intelligence analysts, rewritten in the language of litigation.

  1. State every theory. Yours, the other side’s, and any alternative a factfinder could believe. They can’t all be true, so the evidence has something to decide between.
  2. Grade the evidence. Rate each source from A to D and each piece of information from 1 to 4. The grade becomes a weight, so an anonymous tip never counts like a fire marshal’s report.1
  3. Rate it against every theory. Six marks, from strongly supports to contradicts, entered straight from the keyboard. Elenchus adds up what weighs against each theory and never counts the same fact twice.
  4. Read the result. The strongest theory is the one the record weighs least against, not the one with the most support. Elenchus names the pivotal evidence: the item that, if it fell, would change the outcome. That is what the other side will attack.2
  1. The grades follow the source-reliability and information-credibility scale long used to weigh intelligence reports. Key ideas
  2. Elenchus is an analysis tool and does not give legal advice. Its results are the arithmetic of the ratings and weights you enter; your judgment decides.

V.

Questions presented.

Whether Elenchus gives legal advice.

It doesn’t. Elenchus is an analysis tool. Its results are the arithmetic of the ratings and weights you enter, and your professional judgment always decides.

Whether you need to know the analysis of competing hypotheses.

No. Elenchus speaks the language of litigation: case theories, evidence, arguments and the theory test. A sample case and a short tour show you the method in a few minutes.

Where your cases are stored.

On your computer, in Documents › Elenchus. We never receive them. Back them up like any client file and turn on full-disk encryption (FileVault or BitLocker).

Why your computer asks for confirmation the first time.

Elenchus isn’t yet signed with paid Apple and Microsoft developer certificates, so your system asks you to confirm once. The install guide shows the extra click. Download Elenchus only from this site.

What happens to your cases if Pro ends.

Nothing is deleted. You can still open, read, print and export every case. If you have more than one active case, editing pauses until you renew or keep only one.

Whether Elenchus works offline.

Yes. No account or connection is needed to work. Elenchus Pro checks the license now and then, and keeps working for 30 days without a connection.
