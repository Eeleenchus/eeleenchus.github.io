Billing questions

How do I activate Pro after buying?

Checkout emails you a license key. In Elenchus, open Settings, paste the key under Elenchus Pro and choose Activate. That’s it: the case limit is removed straight away.

Who processes the payment?

Orders are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. They accept cards and other local payment methods, calculate sales tax or VAT for your country and send your receipt. We never see your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from the link in your receipt email or the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and you won’t be charged again.

Do you offer refunds?

Payments are non-refundable, including partial periods. The free plan includes every feature, so you can evaluate Elenchus fully before paying. Billing errors are always refunded. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy; your local consumer law still applies where it can’t be waived.

What happens to my cases if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. Elenchus returns to the free plan: you can open, read, print and export every case. If you have more than one active case, editing pauses until you renew or move cases to the Trash so that only one remains.

Can I move my license to a new computer?

Yes. In Settings on the old computer choose Deactivate, then activate on the new one. Each license works on two computers at a time.

Do prices include tax?

Prices are in US dollars. Any sales tax or VAT that applies in your country is calculated at checkout.
